Abstract. The author has carried out a research paper on how ERP systems have evolved, presenting their
main characteristics, and conducting a debate between the benefits and challenges but also the
opportunities of implementing such a system. Certainly, ERP systems have reached an absolute level of
importance over time, being able to be compared with the backbone of the organizations where they are
implemented. Research of the available specialized literature was carried out to find gaps in relation to the
challenges and opportunities of implementing the three technologies Enterprise Resource Planning,
Internet of Things and Artificial Intelligence in a single global system. After the study, it was concluded that
by implementing a global system that integrates Enterprise Resource Planning, Internet of Things and
Artificial Intelligence but also interconnects all the companies of the same supply flow, organizations can
reach the highest level in terms of efficiency, forecast and monitoring but also perfect flexibility by adding
Robotic Process Automation to the system. The results of this study complement specialized literature and
make an important contribution to the field of study.
Keywords: Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP); Internet of Things (IoT); Artificial Intelligence (AI);
Robotic Process Automation (RPA); Supply Chain; Service Sector, Digital Transformation.

Introduction

The first signs of life for ERP systems appeared in the 1960s when most companies designed,
developed, and implemented applications dedicated to their business model without being able to
be multiplied to other companies or domains (accounting automation, stock control, salary
calculation). These systems were then combined with the procurement of raw materials and
production, thus opening the first doors to Management Resource Planning (MRP).
Over time, this system has evolved to integrate the financial part, sales and HR,
encompassing the entire operational process of an organization, thus offering the possibility that
these systems can be applied in other areas, thus becoming much more complex, renaming itself
Enterprise Resource Planning, the first such systems being born at the end of the 1980s. Starting
with the 1990s, they became more and more popular in large corporations and multinational
companies, adding new modules such as APS (Advance Planning and Scheduling), CRM
(Customer Relationship Management) and SCM (Supply Chain Management). The
implementation of these new modules brought them real success at the world level, and starting in
the 2000s, they opened to a new market for small and medium-sized companies.
The development of ERP systems coincides with the hardware and software development
of computer systems, but also with the informational explosion of the 1990s and the development

Literature review

Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP)
The main two characteristics of primary importance of the contemporary business environment are
competitiveness and dynamism, which lead organizations to struggle for survival due to the
difficulties in planning and managing operational flows. The implementation of an ERP system
becomes the main solution in leading organizations to reach the desired level of competitiveness
and performance, but also of other goals and priorities (Chipriyanova & Chipriyanov, 2022). The
supply chains of all companies were strongly affected both during the Covid-19 pandemic and due
to the crisis resulting from the war in Ukraine, resulting in delays, cancellations and price increases.
Most of the companies have never encountered such crises before, but they will have to survive
from now on and find new methods and means to remain efficient and competitive in the current
global business environment (Andrieș & Ungureanu, 2022). The accelerated development of the
age of technology and information has led companies to an intense rivalry, regardless of their size
and the sector in which they operate, and in the companies' attempt to remain competitive, they
implement ERP systems that improve their performance, the one that attracts new customers and
the maintenance of old customers. Also, in order to remain competitive, it is vital to be able to
make correct and timely decisions, and this is only possible with the help of information that can
be accessed easily and from anywhere, fast and accurate (Cordova et al., 2021). Integrated systems
of the ERP type incorporate the entire set of activities and functionalities of the organizations'
departments (Purchasing, Logistics, Warehouse, Production, Financial and HR), which are
presented in the form of a software or an application, being used to improve and organize processes
of work but also to increase the efficiency and effectiveness of operations (Azhar et al., 2022).
In the vast majority of cases, large companies with a very large and complex operational
and financial volume opt for the implementation of ERP systems, but because it has been observed
over time that they bring added value by creating an operational flow adapted to each individual
organization and the introduction of functional automations in within applications, instead of
manual activities in the past, even small and medium-sized companies are more and more attracted
to such systems (Azhar et al., 2022). Small, medium and large companies can develop sustainably
only if they opt for the implementation of ERP systems that bring about a significant improvement
of financial activities (Barna et al., 2021). Modern management is continuously and constantly
innovated due to the combination of technology and management techniques through these
software tools called ERP systems, together with the professional skills of managers, building
operational processes based on logical and efficient reasoning, offering managers an extraordinary
visibility over the entire operational process of the organization so that it can reach unimaginable
levels in the past of strategy and tactics (Chipriyanova & Chipriyanov, 2022). Modern ERP systems
are becoming more and more integrated, more efficient and more strongly interconnected, but they
also offer multiple options to ensure or increase the safety and security of data because we live in
the era of Cyber Insecurity where there are so many threats to data (McCrevan, 2020).
Although ERP systems offer immense promises and advantages, if the implementation is
not carried out successfully, devastating losses can occur, including the destruction of competitive
advantages, the reduction of organizational performance, even leading to bankruptcy. That is why

According to Azhar et al. (2022) research, incorporating the entire flow of information from
all departments of an organization, it offers companies the opportunity to have a very good realtime visibility of the current situation at any moment, fast, reliable and accurate, being of great help
in making the fairest decisions considering the entire informational ensemble. It also improves and
reduces the necessary time dedicated to communication and transmission of information, thus
creating an interval of free time that can be dedicated to other activities with a much greater value
impact on the organization. On the same page, Vargas and Comuzzi (2020) stated that ERP systems
offer organizations better communication between departments, reduction of stocks and
operational costs, but they also offer much better and prompt reactions to changes in the market.
Cordova et al. (2021) is arguing that over time, experience has revealed a multitude of problems
that have arisen following the implementation of ERP systems, such as the stress level of
employees is much higher than normal, because normal people show resistance to change, lack of
testing corresponding to the systems, problems with the interface or problems with the time zone.
The implementation of ERP systems brings an increased efficiency in the use of resources,
the optimization of the flow of materials, the intensification of the informational flow, the reduction
of the product cost, the improvement of management control over various processes and activities,
and the possibility to further develop and optimize, step by step, the system together with the
operating processes (Chipriyanova & Chipriyanov, 2022). In order to complete a successful ERP
implementation, an effective collaboration between all the departments of the organization is
necessary, as a lot of time and effort is needed, this comes on top of a considerable financial
investment, thus creating an association of this implementation with a risky and very difficult
procedure (Vargas and Comuzzi, 2020).
On the other hand, ERP systems come in time to offer increased confidence to the
organization by providing consistent, accurate and reliable data leading to a much more productive
work environment, reduced operational costs, standardizing, and organizing internal processes to
improve the organization's performance through better productivity and order management (Azhar
et al., 2022).
ERP systems are based on a business model that includes planning, control, and
optimization phases, helping companies to achieve their business goals, simplifying processes, and
providing accurate information, giving companies a competitive advantage in the market by
differentiating themselves from their competitors (Andrieș & Ungureanu, 2022). The
implementation of ERP systems has led to better visibility, transparency and control for top
management, as well as an increase in productivity, a decrease in direct and indirect personnel
costs, and an increase in business figures (Ungureanu, 2022). On the other hand, ERP systems
present difficulties in adapting to specific processes in different organizations, there is also the

Cloud ERP brings added value to businesses by introducing a much stronger flexibility,
namely offering the advantage to organizations to access their data, information and the entire set
of reports and forecasts online, while also continuing to offer the same functions as the ancestors
of traditional ERP. Cloud ERP is presented in the form of three technologies, Software as a Service
(SaaS), Platform as a Service (PaaS) and Infrastructure as a Service (IaaS). Also, the flexibility
brought as added value by Cloud ERP can be divided into economic flexibility by introducing
flexible prices according to the choice of each consumer, flexibility in performance through the
availability of the system anytime and anywhere, flexibility of operational processes because it
introduces the option to vary the necessary resources depending on the volume, and flexibility in
the market, offering the company solutions to perform better than its competitors (Azhar et al.,
2022). An incorrect analysis of the operational processes, the lack of expertise in testing, can cause
major problems in the ERP implementation, as well as the configuration of an inefficient and
inadequate workflow, can cause a decrease in the organization's performance. However, the
business environment in Romania does not favor a successful implementation of ERP systems
because if we look at the Index of the Digital Economy and Society (DESI-2021) which measures
the digital skills of the population, Romania is in last place among the 28 European states
(Ungureanu, 2022).
Internet of Things (IoT)
IoT devices are rapidly disrupting entire business models, it is predicted that they will reach a
market share of 31 billion units by 2025 from 13 billion in 2021 (Olcott, 2022). IoT devices
represent a very complex concept, representing a form of communication, with wireless
connectivity, sensors and codes, where all the objects we currently use are connected to the Internet
and thus can be connected to other devices and machines so that any object from this world can
connect and communicate in an intelligent way (Tucker et al., 2018).
IoT technology has become more and more widespread, more and more devices are
appearing on the market that can be interconnected, gather information, and at the same time offer
a multitude of useful information. Companies invest in tools and processes that will increase
productivity and profits, but ultimately customer satisfaction, so by connecting them with IoT
devices we can increase the volume of data, improving the efficiency of any operational process
(Fryer, 2020). On the same page Tucker et al. (2018) stated that all IoT products can bring unlimited
benefits and it is still a technology at the beginning of the road, the future being boundless,
connecting a smart map and the GPS system car trips are transformed into safer and less stressful
trips, being able to connect from anywhere to a public transport system or order a taxi. The very
rapid development of IoT technology provides an opportunity for innovation and reinvention even
in the energy management sector in any energy consuming or producing sector to adapt to the new
sustainable and smart times (Ma et al., 2021).
IoT technology integrated with traditional ERP systems leads to a much more structured,
consistent and optimized infrastructure from an operational point of view. Inventory planning, asset
utilization and understanding trends are just a few examples where this type of information can
improve operational efficiency. More and more warehouses use Wi-Fi connected devices plus a
camera and a multitude of sensors (“Connected manufacturing with IIoT”, 2020). Tucker et al.
(2018) is arguing that in this period, Cyber-attacks are more and more numerous and stronger, and
in the case of IoT technology, there are more and more worries about data security. On the other

Artificial Intelligence (Al)
Although the term Artificial Intelligence has been introduced in specialized literature since the
1950s, it has recently gained popularity and value due to the development of technologies to
process a very large volume of data and a large coverage of the network with Internet connection.
At this moment, Al has an important palette of applicability in project management, the
introduction of processes in order to reduce risks, assistance in the daily routine of tracking the
project phases, but also the monitoring of the entire flow (Ong and Uddin, 2020).
The era of which we are contemporaries is dominated by the evolution of technology and
certainly the most important innovation is Artificial Intelligence (Al) which produces
transformational effects that spread like an earthquake in practically all fields of society. If we look
more closely at the business environment, Al brings added value through the tools that automate
redundant tasks, that identify data patterns in order to improve process performance by automating
routine processes, but at the same time it also offers new, different and valuable perspectives
(Naidoo & Dulek, 2022). From the very beginning, AI was used to automate specific processes and
repetitive tasks, but over time, companies transitioned to using AI in decision-making, solving
problems, creating products, but also developing the ability of machines to work together with the
people. One of the biggest problems now is the integration of technology within organizations, the
change of processes and organizational culture. On the other hand, the way decisions are made
automatically can disadvantage certain groups, creating social dysfunctions. However, the major
problem is represented by the invasion of personal privacy (Benbya et al., 2020). Over time, Al has
become an essential technology for any business, being able to be implemented in absolutely any
activity sector, leading organizations in a disruptive process both internally and externally (RuizReal et al., 2021).
The performance of traditional Business Intelligence systems will reach new higher levels
by introducing Al technology, because one of the main purposes of this technology is to model the
way people collect data, the way they perceive problems and how they react to solve them, being
designed so that to learn from the input data and make rational decisions (Wang, 2021). Al has a
huge potential to transform businesses and disrupt entire sectors, however companies that want to
integrate this technology face many challenges in finding the sectors where it can bring added value
or which decisions can be automated but also a transformation of the entire organization is
necessary to integrate technology into the workforce (Kerzel, 2021).
Modern logistics systems are supported by very powerful technological networks that
generate a huge volume of data, sensors, machines, systems or intelligent devices, thus leading to
increasing the value of Al technology. Many sectors have shown themselves open to implementing
AI to transform real-time data into immediately actionable decisions (Chien et al., 2020). In his
paper, Hongjin (2021) introduced an artificial intelligence model to identify the risk factors of the
financial supply chain, improving the traditional algorithms to recognize the risk factors by
combining them with IoT functionalities. Integration of Al within organizations brings added value
in improving the efficiency of forecasts so that the entire supply chain can meet the toughest

Methodology

Systematic research of the existing available specialized literature was carried out in relation to the
challenges and opportunities offered by Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP), Internet of Things
(IoT) and Artificial Intelligence (AI).
Research Questions
1. What are the opportunities in using Internet of Things (IoT) versus the challenges to transform
the Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) system of organizations in the service sector?
2. What are the opportunities in using Artificial Intelligence (Al) versus the challenges to transform
the Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) system of organizations in the service sector?
Protocol
To survive in these turbulent times, companies need a technological transformation and this
research debates the opportunities and challenges of using these three technologies ERP, IoT and
Al. Articles related to this subject will be searched in the EBSCO Database and from the list
obtained, only those whose title and abstract are relevant to the research will be selected in a first
phase, and then in a secondary phase those whose text is not relevant will be eliminated. The
information will be systematically collected in close connection with the opportunities and
challenges of using the three technologies together and will be synthesized in a narrative form.
Search of Relevant Articles
An article search was carried out in February and March 2023 using EBSCO Database as a source
(Business Source Complete, EconLit with Full Text, Psychology and behavioral Sciences
Collection, Computer Source, eBook Business Collection, Regional Business News, Library,
Information Science & Technology Abstracts, GreenFILE, Teacher Reference Center, European
Views of the Americans: 1493 to 1750, eBook Collection, eBook Open Access OA Collection).
The search was carried out using as search words “Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP)”
and “Service Sector” than using “Internet of Things (IoT)” and “Enterprise Resource Planning
(ERP)”, continuing with “Artificial Intelligence (Al)” and “Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP)”.
The entire search was filtered on Academic Journals as article type, published between 2020 and
2023 only in English and Romanian.
Selection Criteria
From an initial list of approximately 600 articles, the selection criteria were applied based on title
and abstract as the first phase and then based on the full text as the second phase, finally selecting
25 articles which fir the level of relevance for this research.

However, the implementation and operation of the ERP system largely depends on the data
that is inserted into the platform, information that is entered by people manually, people who are
part of different areas and cultures of the world who may have a high or low level of knowledge
and digital skills, therefore it is necessary to carry out periodic trainings to learn the necessary
skills. Most of the time, in order to provide the best, most accurate and reliable visibility, an ERP
system will require more data to be inserted into the platform, which will lead to the hiring of
additional labor, which is not only an additional cost but also the creation of new processes and
procedures, which will complicate an already complex system. In this phase, the interconnection
between the ERP system and IoT technology will simplify the complexity of the operational
processes, gathering the necessary data and information with the help of sensors, detectors, cameras
and other existing or future technologies that can capture information from the environment.
Moreover, the ERP system can connect with other platforms that have already implemented a
network of objects with IoT technology, for example Google Maps, Weather Forecast, Shipment
Tracking or applications that introduce scanned documents directly into the platform.
While the volume of data included in the platform is getting bigger and bigger, there is a
need for data analysis, extracting and synthesizing relevant data for certain purposes needed in
making reports, forecasts, strategies or decisions. The ability of a platform to understand this
information, analyze it and formulate an answer is given by Artificial Intelligence. Incorporating
AI, together with IoT into the Cloud ERP system of organizations, creating an all-in-one solution,
the solution becomes transformational, offering the best results, companies reaching their
maximum potential, offering enhanced productivity and efficiency, making forecasts with a
percentage of effectiveness much higher but also offering a prompter visibility on the problems
that appear in the operational processes and the implementation of quick solutions. Any successful
organization has a portfolio of clients and suppliers of services, materials or transport, and the
fluidization of the supply chain is fully represented by the interconnection of ERP systems between
the client, supplier, carrier, manufacturer regardless of the size of the flow. Every possible company
uses an ERP system different from the other company, there is a large and competitive market in
this regard, it is almost impossible to have the same platform for everyone, but the creation of a
standard in terms of interconnection between platforms at a global level among all players it is
possible, of course where it is necessary in the form of a "single contact socket". When a company
has signed a contract with a client or supplier, an automated exchange of information between ERP
systems should also be regulated.

Limitations
The selection of the articles was made by one person, the author and not according to the standard
procedure of the systemic research methodology where the selection is made by three people, two
people read and select the articles and the third person destroy all possible ties, due to this fact there
is the possibility of the risk of bias.

Conclusion

Any organization that wants to be efficient and competitive both nationally and internationally will
have to implement an ERP system, but if it wants to go to the next level, to excel in the field in
which it operates, something more is needed, it is necessary to opt for transformational solutions
by implementing an ERP cloud together with IoT and AI.
Moreover, companies can reach the highest level in terms of efficiency, forecasting and
monitoring only by interconnecting the ERP systems of all companies in the same supply chain
from the initial point until the moment the product ends up being used by the final consumer. On
top of that, improving flexibility by integrating RPA (Robotic Process Automation) technology
and adapting ERP systems when they encounter situations that cannot be included in standard
operational flows will be considered the "pearl in the crown".
The results obtained from this research that led to the proposals described in the previous
paragraphs contribute to the field of studies by filling the gaps in the existing specialized literature
being of extraordinary importance and bringing unlimited added value through practical
application in real life cases in absolutely any field or part of the world. It would be interesting to
further research different and multiple concrete flows from the market and the companies that are
part of them in order to research and analyze the data to understand in more detail how the
applicability of the theory proposed above will lead organizations to the highest level in
performance and competitiveness.
