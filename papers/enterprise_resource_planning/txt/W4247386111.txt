Abstrak
Melihat persaingan yang semakin meningkat di industri manufaktur, penerapan
Manajemen Rantai Pasok Berkelanjutan dapat menjadi solusi untuk meningkatkan efisiensi
rantai pasok suatu perusahaan dan juga meningkatkan daya saing dengan para kompetitor. PT.
Elco Indonesia Sejahtera merupakan salah satu perusahaan yang bergerak di bidang industri
penyamakan kulit yang berlokasi di Garut, Jawa Barat. Perusahaan ini belum memiliki sistem
untuk mendukung penerapan Manajemen Rantai Pasok Berkelanjutan dalam proses bisnis
perusahaannya sampai saat ini, termasuk untuk bagian pengadaan. Selain itu, perusahaan juga
belum memiliki system untuk memantau proses bisnisnya. Oleh karena itu, penelitian ini
bertujuan untuk merancang sistem informasi terintegrasi untuk bagian pengadaan dengan
menggunakan sistem yang berbasis Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP), serta melakukan
perancangan dashboard monitoring agar proses bisnis dapat lebih efektif, efisien, dan tentunya
mampu mengelola Manajemen Rantai Pasok Berkelanjutan dengan baik. Hasil dari penelitian
ini berupa rancangan sistem dan juga dashboard monitoring yang berbasis ERP untuk
mendukung penerapan Manajemen Rantai Pasok Berkelanjutan.
Kata kunci—Manajemen Rantai Pasok Berkelanjutan, Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP),
Pengadaan Berkelanjutan

Abstract
Seeing the increasing competition in the manufacturing industry, the implementation of
Sustainable Supply Chain Management (SSCM) can be a solution to improve the efficiency of a
company's supply chain and also increase competitiveness with competitors. PT. Elco Indonesia
Sejahtera is a company engaged in the leather tanning industry located in Garut, West Java.
This company does not yet have a system to support the implementation of Sustainable Supply
Chain Management in its business processes, including for the procurement department. In
addition, the company also does not have a system to monitor its business processes. Therefore,
this study aims to design an integrated information system for the procurement department
using a system based on Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP), as well as to design a monitoring
dashboard so that business processes can be more effective, efficient, and of course, able to
manage SSCM well. The results of this study are in the form of a system design and a
monitoring dashboard based on ERP to support the implementation of SSCM.
Keywords— Sustainable Supply Chain Management (SSCM), Enterprise Resource Planning
(ERP), Sustainable Procurement

1. INTRODUCTION

T

he Central Statistics Agency recorded the growth of several manufacturing
industry sectors in the second quarter of 2018. Production growth increased by
4.36% compared to the second quarter of 2017. The types of manufacturing that
experienced the highest production growth were the leather and leather goods industry which
reached 27, 73%. There are many leather-producing areas in Indonesia, one of which is in Garut
Regency, West Java. The Garut Regency Industry, Trade, Cooperative and SME Service (2017)
states that the high population and production capacity of livestock in Garut Regency initiated
the growth and development of the leather tanning industry in Garut Regency. The impact that
resulted from the economic side was very much felt by the existence of this leather tanning
industry. But besides that, this also raises a negative issue because the waste produced can
threaten the environmental ecosystem [1]. Therefore, the industry must implement Sustainable
Supply Chain Management (SSCM) practices that help the industry reduce its environmental
issues and increase its sustainable activities by considering aspects of green innovation [2].
Sustainable Supply Chain Management (SSCM) is the relationship between the supply chain
where three economic, environmental and social factors are considered in its operations [3]. The
environmental aspect of SSCM aims to minimize or eliminate waste in hazardous chemicals,
emissions, energy and waste along the supply chain process [4].
SSCM involves entities from the procurement cycle to the production cycle to the
distribution cycle with different interests but having the main objective of meeting the needs of
the end consumers [5]. Therefore, procurement plays a vital role in SSCM activities in
companies, such as what happened to PT Elco Indonesia Sejahtera, a leather tanning industry in
Garut, West Java, Indonesia. The procurement department of PT Elco Indonesia Sejahtera has a
significant role in supply chain activities because all raw material purchasing activities required
for the production process are carried out in procurement. So that management and monitoring
in procurement are needed to support the implementation of SSCM in the procurement section
of PT Elco Indonesia Sejahtera. The indicator that can be used to apply SSCM to procurement is
the sustainable procurement indicator. The Head of the Construction Development Agency of
the Ministry of Public Works explained that sustainable procurement is a process of procuring
goods that is carried out by assessing the entire construction process starting from planning,
construction and service life (life cycle cost), there by providing economic and social benefits
and reducing negative impacts to the environment.
Currently, PT Elco Indonesia Sejahtera does not have a system to support SSCM
implementation. Therefore, to improve the company's performance, PT Elco Indonesia
Sejahtera needs to develop an information system with integrated data so that business
processes can be more effective, efficient, and of course, able to manage SSCM well. To solve
these problems, we need a system that can integrate and monitor company business processes
using an Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) system. ERP is a system that can integrate all
company supply chains and business processes by using a database in one place [6]. PT Elco
Indonesia Sejahtera also does not have a monitoring system for the procurement business
process, so this research will also design a monitoring dashboard. The dashboard is an
information system application model to present performance quality information from a
company or organizational institution [7]. This study aims to help the leather tanning industry to
apply environmentally friendly criteria to create sustainable supply chain management in the
procurement department using an ERP system.

2. METHODOLOGY
In implementing a sustainable procurement system, this study uses the QuickStart
methodology. This methodology has three phases, as shown in Figure 1. The first phase is a
kick-off call, which in this phase defines the company's business processes, especially in the
procurement section. The second phase is analysis. At this stage, an analysis of the company's
existing business processes will be carried out, then determine the target business processes that
will be applied to the system. Then the third phase, namely Configuration, is the stage for
configuring and adjusting the system.

Figure 1. System Development Method
2.1

Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP)
Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) is the software program used by companies to
integrate and coordinate information on their business areas in real-time [8]. ERP aims to help
manage every resource in the company. These resources include money, people, machines, time
and materials [9]. In principle, with an ERP system, the industry or company can run optimally
and can reduce inefficient operational costs, such as inventory costs and losses due to technical
errors [6].
2.2

Sustainable Supply Chain Management
Sustainable Supply Chain Management (SSCM) is simply defined as the relationship
between business supply chains where three economic, environmental and social factors are
considered in its operational activities [3]. The environmental aspect of SSCM aims to minimize
or eliminate waste in hazardous chemicals, emissions, energy and waste along the supply chain
process where the supply chain process starts from product design, material selection,

2.3

Sustainable Procurement
Sustainable procurement is known as the environmental purchasing approach, which
ensures that the items purchased are in line with the desired ecological attributes, such as
reusable, recyclable, and contain non-toxic materials. Sustainable procurement is also related to
material substitution through the right source of raw materials to reduce waste by minimizing
hazardous waste, thus requiring good cooperation with suppliers to develop environmentally
friendly products. Sustainable procurement focuses on cooperating with suppliers to create
products that are environmentally sustainable [10].
2.4

Odoo
Odoo is the world's most accessible all-in-one management software. Odoo has several
characteristics, such as customizable, comprehensive, modular and open-source [11]. Because it
is an open-source application, users can freely customize it according to their individual
preferences. Odoo's strengths include providing various business functions and an easy-tounderstand user interface. Odoo has many modules that users can download and use
immediately. Most of all, the essential modules can be downloaded for free. Some of these
modules are manufacturing, inventory, sales, inventory, procurement, etc [12].
2.5

Dashboard
The dashboard is defined as a visual display of the most critical information needed to
achieve one or more goals, which are combined and arranged in one screen so that they can be
monitored at a glance. The dashboard provides a display interface with various forms such as
diagrams, reports, visual indicators, and alert mechanisms combined with dynamic and relevant
[13]. The concept of the performance dashboard has been around for many years, and the
dashboard has been widely adopted by companies or businesses [11].

3. RESULT AND DISCUSSION
This study discusses problems regarding the procurement business process in the leather
tanning industry, which in carrying out its business processes have not applied environmentally
friendly indicators. In addition, business activities are still carried out manually, are not
integrated and cannot be monitored in real-time. So it is necessary to implement an ERP-based
system that can help automate and integrate business processes in the company. This research
will design a sustainable procurement system based on ERP and also a system that can display
data in the form of reports to simplify the process of monitoring company performance and
performance in the form of a dashboard.

process of the procurement department will be determined. This stage aims to understand the
company's business processes so that it can determine the system implementation plan.

Figure 6 shows a visualization of the proposed procurement dashboard. Several data
will be displayed, including supplier data, percentage of green material, percentage of
dangerous materials, delivery time, supplier rating, cost of purchase orders, cost reduction and
cost savings by year and supplier category. Supplier data displays information about the number
of suppliers contained in the parent company data. Then it is classified into two groups, namely
suppliers that are ISO 14000 certified and those that are not.
Then there is data that displays information about the percentage of green material. The
higher the percentage indicates that the materials used by the company are environmentally
friendly because these materials are reusable, recyclable, and biodegradable. So that the waste
management process becomes more manageable. In addition, there is information on the
percentage of dangerous materials. The lower the percentage indicates that the less use of
materials contaminated with hazardous materials so that it has a good impact on the company.
Then there is information about the delivery time. This data is analyzed by comparing
suppliers. The indicators used are early, on time, and late delivery. Based on Figure 6, it can be
seen that supplier 4 has better performance than other suppliers because it is never late in
making deliveries. Even though the percentage of supplier 1 is higher, there is still a percentage
of late delivery.
There is also a supplier rating, which contains information about the three suppliers
with the best ratings. Then there is information that displays all purchase costs with a five-year
trend indicator. Then there are two other metrics that actually convey the same information but
are expressed in two different ways: cost reduction and cost savings. This is analyzed based on
five-year trends and by supplier category. It plays a key role in the procurement department, as
it helps measure the large savings realized over time and helps in monitoring the financial
performance of the purchasing function. Companies can use this figure because it has a direct
impact on profits and losses.

4. CONCLUSION
Based on the results of the research that has been done, it can be concluded that the
development of an ERP-based sustainable procurement system aims to create an
environmentally friendly business process procurement information system. Companies can
select suppliers that have been certified environmentally-friendly and choose the desired
material criteria in the system. Then the existing data will be displayed in the form of a
dashboard. This can help companies analyze current business processes and help make
decisions. The result of this research is a system design for ERP-based procurement business
processes for the needs of the leather tanning industry. The application of sustainable indicators
can assist companies in creating an environmentally friendly industry.

5. SUGGESTION
Based on the research results, it is suggested that if there is further research on this
industry, it is hoped that further researchers will be able to explore a broader range of Key
Performance Indicators as environmentally friendly attributes.
